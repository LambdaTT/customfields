💬 Commentaries:

Aligns this module with the IAM permissions-domain simplification (IAM v1.3.0) by adopting the
tag-based permissioning standard.

---

✨ Added:

    ▸ Seed `dbseeds/1795316470_customfields-permissions.php`, registering the module's permission
      tags in `IAM_PERMISSION`: `customfields.customfield.read` and
      `customfields.customfield.manage`.

---

🔧 Changed:

    ▸ Replaced the entity/CRUD permission codes (`STT_SETTINGS_CUSTOMFIELD` C/D) with tag-based
      permissions enforced via `iam/permission->requireAny()`. Creating and deleting a field now
      require `customfields.customfield.manage`.

    ▸ Module presence is now checked via `ModsManager::modExists('iam')`.

---

🔧 Fixed:

    ▸ Removed the call to `iam/permission->validatePermissions()`, a method dropped in the IAM
      refactor that would otherwise throw an "undefined method" fatal on every guarded endpoint.
